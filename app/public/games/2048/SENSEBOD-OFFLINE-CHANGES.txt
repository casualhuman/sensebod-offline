The files come from the top of the repository, which runs as it is (the
project's Sass source and Rakefile were not used, and nothing was built or
downloaded).

Why it was changed: the game is played with no internet, inside a sandboxed
frame with no storage, on Android 8 phones (Chrome 58) with a 320px touch
screen. The frame is 284 x 409 px there and nothing may scroll.

Files that are unchanged
------------------------
- All ten files in js/ (the game itself, its swipe and keyboard input, its
  three small polyfills).
- LICENSE.txt.
- style/fonts/ClearSans-Regular-webfont.woff and ClearSans-Bold-webfont.woff.

Files left out
--------------
- favicon.ico and the meta/ folder (home-screen icon and two iPhone start-up
  pictures): not used inside a frame.
- The Clear Sans Light font (not used by the game) and the .eot and .svg
  copies of all three fonts (for browsers older than the phones' Chrome).
- style/main.scss, style/helpers.scss, style/fonts/clear-sans.css, Rakefile,
  README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md, .jshintrc, .gitignore.

Changes to index.html
---------------------
- Added: sensebod-offline-shim.js as the first script (in-memory stand-in
  for localStorage, sessionStorage and cookies; copied from the Hextris
  folder of Sensebod Offline). The game then keeps the board and the best
  score in memory: they are forgotten when the game is closed.
- Added: sensebod-offline-layout.js (see below).
- Removed: the "How to play" paragraph, the "official version" note with its
  link to git.io, and the credits paragraph with its links to
  gabrielecirulli.com, the iTunes store and asherv.com.
- The credit is kept as plain text: "Game by Gabriele Cirulli".
- The line above the board now reads "Swipe or use arrow keys. Join numbers
  to get 2048!" (it said "Join the numbers and get to the 2048 tile!").
- Removed: the favicon, apple-touch-icon and start-up image links and the
  Apple / "HandheldFriendly" / "MobileOptimized" meta tags. The viewport tag
  lost "target-densitydpi" and "minimal-ui" (both no longer understood).
- "New Game" now comes before the hint in the page source (it floats right).

style/main.css (rewritten)
--------------------------
- Kept from the project: all colours, the tile colour rules, and the
  "appear", "pop", "move-up" and "fade-in" animations.
- New layout: one compact column for every screen size. Title and scores in
  a 44px row, hint + credit + a 48px high "New Game" button in the next row,
  then the board. The project's two fixed layouts (a 500px board for
  desktops, a 280px board inside a 320px page for phones) are gone: neither
  fits a 284px frame.
- The page cannot scroll: html and body are the size of the frame, overflow
  hidden, body fixed, touch-action none, no text selection.
- "Try again" and "Keep going" are 48px high.
- The font rules now sit in this file (two weights, .woff only); there is no
  @import any more.
- The glow around tiles from 128 upwards is smaller (12px blur in place of
  30px) to suit the smaller tiles.
- Nothing newer than Chrome 58 understands: no flexbox "gap", no "inset",
  no "aspect-ratio", no CSS variables, no min() / max().

sensebod-offline-layout.js (new, plain ES5)
-------------------------------------------
- Measures the frame and writes the size of the board, cells and tiles, the
  tile positions and the number sizes into a <style> element. The board is
  the largest square that fits under the two header rows: 276px in a
  284 x 409 frame, 247px in a 284 x 360 frame, never more than the
  project's own 500px.
- Does it again on "resize", "orientationchange", "load" and four times a
  second for the first ten seconds, because a sandboxed frame can be 0 x 0
  for a moment while it loads. Until the frame has a size it uses the
  284 x 409 sizes.
- Cancels "touchmove" on the whole page (asked for as a non-passive
  listener) so the page cannot scroll or bounce under a swipe; cancels the
  long-press menu.

style/fonts/LICENSE-ClearSans.txt (new)
---------------------------------------
- The copyright and licence statement found inside the two font files,
  followed by the text of the Apache License 2.0. The repository states the
  licence inside the font files but does not carry the licence text; the
  text was copied from another Apache-2.0 package already on the build
