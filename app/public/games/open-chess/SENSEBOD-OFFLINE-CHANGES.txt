Open Chess (Chess AI) in Sensebod Offline
=========================================

Date of these changes: 2026-11-16

The project runs as it is (no build). On its own page it loads jQuery,
chessboard.js, Bootstrap, Font Awesome and Google's icon font from the
internet. Those that are needed were copied in from their official
repositories; nothing was built and no code from any repository was run.

  from the Chess AI repository. The licence text is at the top of the file
  and in licenses/chessjs-LICENSE.txt (from https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js,
  commit d43e6683efeefbd07f8c53e8e7a47c62cf612439).
- js/chessboard-1.0.0.min.js and css/chessboard-1.0.0.min.css: chessboard.js
  1.0.0 by Chris Oakman, MIT. Taken unchanged from the release archive
  website/releases/chessboardjs-1.0.0.zip in https://github.com/oakmac/chessboardjs
  (commit bfb9315cd44d22c4a3ccc883ab4c0b42e902557e). Both files have the same
  SHA-384 sums as the ones the Chess AI page asked for from the internet.
  Licence: licenses/chessboardjs-LICENSE.md.
- js/jquery-3.5.1.min.js: jQuery 3.5.1, MIT, unchanged, from dist/ at the tag

  file as in the other games.

Files left out
--------------
- img/chesspieces/wikipedia/*.png (the twelve piece pictures). They are the
  same files as in chessboard.js, and neither repository states a licence for
  them (the MIT licence files speak of the software; the folder name points
  to Wikipedia, with no author and no licence named). They were NOT copied.
  js/pieces.js draws twelve new, simple pieces instead (written for Sensebod
  Offline, MIT like the game).
- Bootstrap (CSS and JS), Font Awesome and Google's Material Icons: all came
  from the internet. A small hand-written css/main.css replaces the layout;
  no icon was in use.
- css/main.css of the project (replaced), README.md, .gitattributes.

Changes to index.html (rewritten)
---------------------------------
- Every address on another host is gone (5 scripts and style sheets from
  CDNs, their integrity attributes, the link to GitHub in the footer). The
  credit "Chess AI (c) 2020 Zhang Zeyu, MIT licence" stays as text.
- Scripts are plain <script> tags at the end of the body, the storage
  stand-in first.
- The page is now: one status line, the board, three buttons (New game, Undo,
  Level) and the credit line. Removed: "positions evaluated" figures, the
  Settings, Opening Positions and Computer vs. Computer panels, the search
  depth for White, "Show Suggested Move", the Advantage bar, Redo. They need
  a large screen or make an old phone work for nothing.

Changes to js/main.js
---------------------
Kept from the project: the piece tables, evaluateBoard() and minimax(), and
the idea of the whole game. Changed:
- Newer JavaScript written the old way (10 ** 10, [a, b] = ..., `text ${x}`,
  includes()), although Chrome 58 would have accepted these.
- Level button instead of the depth lists: Easy = depth 1 (the default),
  Medium = depth 2, Hard = depth 3. Depths 4 and 5 are gone.
- minimax(): the depth is tested before the list of moves is made (same
  answer, much less work at the leaves); a search that has run for 4 seconds
  stops and plays the best move among those it looked at fully.
- evaluateBoard(): asks in_draw() once instead of also asking for threefold
  repetition and stalemate again (in_draw() already includes both).
- The learner always plays White. The status line always says whose turn it
  is ("Your turn (White).", "The computer is thinking...", "Check! ...") or
  how the game ended (checkmate for either side, the kinds of draw).
- Bug fixed: "if (!checkStatus('black'));" (a stray semicolon) made the
  computer try to move after the game had ended, which stopped the script.
- makeBestMove() plays the move first and scores it after, like the
  learner's move.
- Tap to move, as well as drag and drop: tap a white piece (it is marked,
  green dots show where it may go, red frames show what it may take), then
  tap one of the marked squares. Tap the piece again to let it go. This also
  works with a mouse. The grey "hover" marks of the project are gone (a
  finger does not hover).
- Touch: a finger on the board never scrolls the page (touch-action: none,
  and touchstart / touchmove are cancelled on the board); chessboard.js's
  own touchmove listener is added as non-passive.
- Undo takes back the computer's move and the learner's move, also stops a
  computer move that is still waiting, and puts the evaluation sum back.
  The button is greyed out when there is nothing to undo. alert() is no
  longer used (a sandboxed frame shows no alert box).
- New game asks for a second tap ("Tap again") while a game is being played,
  because confirm() does not work in a sandboxed frame either.
- The board is sized to the frame (made for 284 x 409 and 284 x 360), waits
  until the frame has a size before it is built, and is resized when the
  frame changes. The a-h / 1-8 marks in the squares are off (too small to
  read on these squares).
- The pieces come from js/pieces.js as data: pictures (no picture files).
- A pawn that reaches the last row always becomes a queen (as in the project).

Nothing here uses localStorage, cookies, eval or the network.
