# Sensebod round 9 — Style B cast IN SCENES (Child protection)

## What the owner wants
Round 8 (`round8/CPB-*.dc.html`) used the diverse cast (`cast/*.svg.txt`, sheet `project/CPB-Cast.dc.html`) but only as head-and-shoulders portraits. The owner preferred how the earlier flat boards (`project/T-CP-Parent.dc.html`, `project/T-CP-Elder.dc.html`, `project/V3-CP-*.dc.html`) told a STORY in each picture: people doing something in a real place — at home, at a school desk, in the village, at a signpost, at the barray. Now combine both: **the Style B cast characters, drawn half- or full-body, acting inside detailed, lively scenes of Sierra Leonean village and town life.** "Motion": people walking, pointing, kneeling, talking, listening, sitting at a desk, holding hands, waving — not standing stiffly.

## Characters
Use the 13-person cast exactly as specified in ROUND8-BRIEF.md and drawn in `cast/<id>.svg.txt` (faces, hair, headwear, glasses, hearing aid, clothing colours, skin tones). Reuse each cast head drawing (copy its face/hair/head group, scale/translate it with a `transform`) and draw the body in the same Style B look: clean dark-brown outlines (~1.5–2px at scene scale), cel shading (base/shadow/highlight, light from top-left), natural proportions (children clearly smaller than adults; elders slightly stooped with a stick where it fits), hands and simple feet/sandals. Keep every character recognisable and consistent across scenes. Original characters only.

## Scenes — shared look (all three illustrators use this so the section feels like one world)
- Card scene viewBox `0 0 360 200` (wide), header scenes `0 0 390 220`, tablet hero scenes may be `0 0 720 300`. Each scene `role="img"` with an `aria-label` describing who is doing what where.
- Environment palette (muted, so the people stand out): sky #E8F0F9 with soft white clouds and a pale sun #FBE3A1; distant hills #CFDDEB; ground/path warm earth #E6D6BF with darker #D2BC9C texture strokes; house walls #F6F1EA / #EDE4D6 with shadow #D9CBB6; zinc roofs #A9B8C8 with ridge lines #8FA1B4; wooden doors and benches #8A5A3E / #6E4630; window frames #085CAA; school building with blue trim #085CAA and a dark blackboard #2E3B36; a simple police post sign and a clinic sign as blank or "[ ]" placeholder boards (no real logos/insignia); fences, water bucket, mats, school bags, books, a bench — small believable props. Small amounts of other colour only in props and clothing; no gradients except subtle ones inside `<defs>`.
- **No trees, palms or big foliage on any Child protection screen** (owner's earlier instruction). Grass tufts at most.
- Calm, safe, respectful; nothing frightening; no depiction of harm. Sensitive topics are shown through the supportive moment (a child talking to a trusted adult, a parent listening), never the harm itself.
- Inline SVG only; ids unique per board (prefix with board + scene name). Keep each board < 220 KB; simplify detail if needed.
- First write each scene to `scenes/<board>-<slot>.svg.txt`, then place it into the board (inline). You MAY render your boards/scenes in a scratch browser/headless Chrome to check faces, bodies and composition — do so and fix anything that looks wrong (eyes misplaced, limbs odd, overlaps). Do not publish.

## Board rules
Base each board on its current version (`round8/CPB-*.dc.html` for layout/copy/links; ROUND6 lesson model; V3 light UI; quick-exit "Leave this page", Back, Home, Listen; contact placeholders). Change only the pictures and the minimum layout needed (picture areas can grow, e.g. 170–190px tall on phone cards, 220–260 on tablet). Root size == `$preview`; report exact sizes. Format rules: ROUND2-BRIEF.md.
