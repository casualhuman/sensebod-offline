Where should you build a quail house?

Default

Chosen

Correct, with reason

Correct!

In a dry place, out of the wind. Quail stay healthy when the house is dry and the wind cannot blow in.

Not quite. Try again.

On wet ground near the river. Wet ground makes quail sick. Look for a dry place.
