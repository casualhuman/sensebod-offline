Portal tile · picture, badge, title, one line

1 · Picture On a light tint panel, inset 6px inside the card. Blue tints; green tints for Agriculture only.

2 · Icon badge 56px circle, white 3px icon, overlaps the picture edge.

3 · Title 19px, weight 800, ink. Two lines are fine.

4 · One line 15px, weight 600, slate #4A5568 (7.53:1 on white). The whole tile is one tap target.

Tap boundary rule: portal tiles, topic tiles, person cards and quiz answers are whole-card taps, so they show a 2px border of 3:1 or more: blue #6B87AD (3.43:1 on page) or green #4F8A5F (3.81:1 on farm page); the Agriculture portal tile uses #1F7A3A (5.01:1). Static panels (Continue, notes, sign-in panels) use a light decorative border and carry their action as a solid button inside.
