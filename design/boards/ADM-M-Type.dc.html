Lesson text

Dirty water can make people sick. Germs that we cannot see live in water from rivers, ponds and open wells.

You can make water safer at home. Keep it in a clean container with a lid, off the ground and away from animals.

Three things to do every day

  • Boil the water, then let it cool with the lid on.
  • Pour water out. Do not dip a cup inside.
  • Wash the container with soap and clean water every week.

Clean water keeps your family strong and helps children stay in school.
