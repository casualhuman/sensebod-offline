Sensebod cast — Agriculture

Eight original characters for the farm animal videos: poultry and rabbit keepers, a dairy herder, a market trader, a young mother, an extension officer and two boys who help. Same Style B look as the other casts: clean dark-brown outlines, shading lit from the top left, big friendly eyes.
