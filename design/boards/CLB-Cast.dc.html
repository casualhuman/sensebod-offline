Sensebod cast — Climate

Eight original characters for the Climate lessons: farmers, a fisherman, children, a grandmother, a flood volunteer and a radio presenter. Same Style B look as the Child protection cast: clean dark-brown outlines, three-tone shading lit from the top left, big friendly eyes.

The cast

Ages 10 to about 70

Pa Foday

Rice farmer · about 55

Has farmed the swamp rice fields all his life and reads the sky every morning.

id: pa-foday

Mama Kumba

Vegetable farmer and market trader · about 45

Grows greens and peppers and sells them at the market; always practical.

id: mama-kumba

Sahr

Fisherman · about 30

Paddles the river every day and notices when the water changes.

id: sahr

Hawa

School climate-club member · age 14

Leads the school climate club and organises clean-ups.

id: hawa

Musa

Curious boy · age 10

Asks "why?" about everything, especially the weather.

id: musa

Ya Sento

Grandmother · about 70

Remembers many rainy seasons; walks with a stick and tells stories.

id: ya-sento

Idrissa

Community flood volunteer · about 28

Checks the river level and helps families move to higher ground.

id: volunteer-idrissa

Mabinty

Local radio presenter · about 35

Reads the weather news on community radio and answers questions.

id: presenter-mabinty
