What to do if a child tells you

Four steps. Do them in this order.

  1. 1 Step 1: Listen Stay calm. Let the child speak. Do not rush them.
  2. 2 Step 2: Believe Say: “I believe you. It is not your fault.”
  3. 3 Step 3: Keep them safe Make sure the child is safe now. Do not promise to keep it a secret.
