Talking about safety at home

Children feel safe when they know they can talk to you about anything, big or small. This note gives you a few simple ways to start.

Being read now: Choose a calm, ordinary moment to talk, for example while you cook or walk to the market. Short talks, often, work better than one big talk.

Three things you can do this week

  1. 1Use the real names for parts of the body, so your child can tell you clearly if something is wrong.
  2. 2Agree on trusted adults. Your child can come to you, and to two more grown-ups you both trust.
  3. 3Listen calmly. If your child tells you something, stay calm and say: “Thank you for telling me. You did the right thing.”

If you are ever worried about a child, you do not have to handle it alone. The Get help page shows who you can talk to.
